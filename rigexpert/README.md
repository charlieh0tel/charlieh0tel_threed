# RigExpert Stick Extended Cover — 20 mm Longer Strap

A small remix of **“RigExpert Stick (all models) extended cover with
strap”** on Printables.

## Original design and credit

**All credit for the original cover and strap design belongs to the
creator of the original Printables model:**

https://www.printables.com/model/1240883-rigexpert-stick-all-models-extended-cover-with-str

Please visit the original listing for the designer's name, original
files, and print guidance. This remix is based on that design, and the
changes are described below.

## Changes

- Lengthened the straight section of the integrated strap by **20 mm**.
- Kept the cover dimensions, attachment geometry, and strap
  cross-section unchanged.
- Arranged the model diagonally to fit a **Prusa MK3.5S**
  (250 × 210 mm) build plate.
- Approximate X/Y footprint: **235.3 × 197.1 mm**.

## Printing

File: `rigexpert-stick-strap-plus-20mm-mk3.5s.3mf`

The model is intended to fit the MK3.5S bed **without a brim or skirt**.
Confirm the placement in your slicer before printing. Use the original
designer's material and print settings as a starting point.
